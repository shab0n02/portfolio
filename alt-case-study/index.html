Offer design

I packaged the service so buyers could pick an entry point.

ALTO is four productized tiers, from a small automation to a full systems rebuild. Pick a tier to see its scope.

Smallest entry pointLargest engagement
  1. Step 1Diagnose
  2. Step 2Design
  3. Step 3Deliver
  4. Step 4Sustain
  • A named acronym, ALTO, so prospects can place themselves before the first call.
  • The same four-step delivery method on every tier, so scope reads the same at any size.
  • Two price books: USD for international buyers and a local book for Pakistan, with rules for which applies.
  • A paid consult fee that qualifies buyers before any scoping work, plus competitor research on Pakistani and US studios to set the price bands.
