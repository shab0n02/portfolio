Outbound playbook

How I build an outbound motion from zero to pipeline: documented, tested and repeatable.

Motion
Multi-channel outbound
Channels
LinkedIn, email, cold call
Tools
Sales Navigator, Apollo, Lusha, Dripify
Pilot batch
100 accounts before automation
Method
Signal-led, ICP-filtered, A/B tested
  1. Days 1 to 2

    Product deep-dive

    Document the MVP and USP: what problem it solves, who it's for, and what makes it defensible. No outreach until the value fits in one sentence.

  2. Week 1

    Build the ICP with marketing

    Firmographics, technographics, buying triggers and persona pain points, checked against existing customer data before it's locked.

  3. Week 1

    AI-assisted ICP filtering

    Tighten the filters, remove noise, surface edge cases and test against real account data. The result is a sharper ICP ready for tooling.

  4. Weeks 1 to 2

    Lead lists in Sales Navigator

    Boolean filters, titles, company size, tech stack and growth signals. Saved searches for a steady feed and a first lead list.

  5. Week 2

    Signal audit of 100 accounts

    Research each account by hand: job changes, growth, content activity, and filings through Companies House UK or the SEC for enterprise targets.

  6. Week 2

    Copy and A/B tests

    Variants for subject lines, openers and CTAs, tested on the pilot batch. No message scales until it's proven.

  7. Weeks 2 to 3

    First 100 to 150 by hand

    Manual sends keep quality high and show what lands. Automation is earned, not assumed.

  8. Week 3 onward

    Automate with Dripify and Lusha

    Dripify runs the sequences and Lusha supplies verified emails, while I work replies, live calls and the active pipeline.

  9. Ongoing

    The multi-touch cadence

    Timing, channel order and messaging adjust to engagement data.

    • Connect and email 1
    • Bridge and email 2
    • Cold call
    • DM and email 3
