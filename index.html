Outbound playbook

How I build an outbound motion from zero to pipeline: documented, tested and repeatable.

Motion
Multi-channel outbound
Channels
LinkedIn, email, cold call
Tools
Sales Navigator, Apollo, Lusha, Dripify, Outreach
Pilot batch
100 accounts before automation
Method
Signal-led, ICP-filtered, A/B tested
  1. Days 1 to 2

    Product deep-dive and documentation

    The first day goes entirely to understanding the product. I document the MVP and USP: what problem it solves, who it's for, and what makes it defensible. No outreach starts until I can state the value in one sentence.

  2. Week 1

    Build the ICP with marketing

    Work with marketing on an ideal customer profile: firmographics, technographics, buying triggers and persona pain points. Check it against existing customer data before locking it in.

  3. Week 1

    AI-assisted ICP filtering

    Run the ICP through AI to tighten the filters, remove noise, surface edge cases and test it against real account data. The output is a sharper ICP ready for tooling.

  4. Weeks 1 to 2

    Saved searches and lead lists in Sales Navigator

    Apply the ICP in LinkedIn Sales Navigator with boolean filters, job titles, company size, tech stack and growth signals. Build saved searches for a steady feed and export a first lead list.

  5. Week 2

    Signal audit of the first 100 accounts

    Research the first 100 accounts one by one. Pull buying signals from Sales Navigator, such as job changes, growth and content activity, and cross-check filings through Companies House UK or the SEC for enterprise targets.

  6. Week 2

    Draft copy and A/B test messaging

    Write A/B variants for each touchpoint: subject lines, opening lines and CTAs. Test them on the pilot batch, track reply rates and sentiment, and iterate. No message scales until it's proven.

  7. Weeks 2 to 3

    Send the first 100 to 150 by hand

    Before any automation, the first 100 to 150 touches go out manually. That keeps quality high, shows where the messaging catches, and builds real signal on what lands. Automation is earned, not assumed.

  8. Week 3 onward

    Automate with Dripify and Lusha

    Once the messaging works, Dripify runs the sequences at scale and Lusha supplies verified emails. Automation runs while I focus on replies, live calls and the active pipeline.

  9. Ongoing

    The full multi-touch cadence

    Every prospect moves through a structured multi-channel cadence. Timing, channel order and messaging keep adjusting based on engagement data.

    • LinkedIn connect and email 1
    • LinkedIn bridge and email 2
    • Cold call
    • LinkedIn DM and email 3
20%Organic growth in the first 30 days

I ran this playbook for an international manufacturing consulting firm in the M&A integration sector. ICP development, signal-led research, A/B-tested messaging and the multi-touch cadence drove about 20% organic growth in outreach engagement and qualified pipeline within the first 30 days.
